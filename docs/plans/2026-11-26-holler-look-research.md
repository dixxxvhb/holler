# Holler look: research digest (2026-11-26, Sonnet research pass, sources in the session)

- Identity in this category is one constraint, not a full theme: Instagram Close Friends = one green ring/star;
  Locket = its widget; Retro = the weekly friend card + filmstrip. Holler should own one structural idea.
- Marco Polo moved to higher contrast for legibility. Snapchat's 2025 simplified 3-tab redesign was reverted
  after backlash: keep the record button and the strip where people expect them.
- Calm camera: iOS 26 Camera hides controls behind pop-outs (calm but slower); iPhone 16 Camera Control can
  hide all chrome. Good rule: while recording, fade everything but stop, timer, and the tools usable mid-take.
- Rooms: Telegram's per-chat themes (animated 3 to 4 color gradients, patterns, day/night). Apply room color
  to the home list, strip, chrome edges, player bars; never tint the video. Ambient motion off the video.
- Invites: link + QR + manual code; link carries the code; don't toast "copied" when share is dismissed
  (AbortError). Idea: the code as a ticket/pass in the room color, big letters in separate cells.
- Fonts (Google): Fraunces (warm wonky display, SOFT/WONK axes), Gabarito (rounded geometric), Figtree (warm
  body), Familjen Grotesk (ink traps), DM Mono (codes/timers). Bricolage is Partiful's stand-in: moving off it
  helps Holler look like itself.
- iPhone pitfalls: big backdrop blur over live video costs frames (prefer solid/semi-opaque surfaces + a
  bottom-weighted scrim); safe areas in standalone PWAs shift by iOS version; text-shadow alone fails contrast
  on video; 44pt targets on the rail even with small glyphs.
