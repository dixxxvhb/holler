  • A chat with one person leads with their face and is named for them. A group leads with its name and everyone's faces. You can tell them apart before you read a word.
  • Each section keeps its order, so Maya is always where Maya was. A chat with something new grows taller in place and shows the count and a play button, as it does today.
  • A game waiting on you now says which game, not "a game is waiting".
  • New chat and Join with a code move into the one plus button. The bottom of the screen becomes the three places.

Today: one list of chats, reordered by whoever spoke last, with two buttons pinned across the bottom.

4 · Inside a chat

The chat is something you can read

  • The whole back-and-forth runs down the screen, newest at the bottom. Theirs on the left, yours on the right, the same tail corners the app already uses.
  • Notes are readable where they sit. No more opening a tiny tile to read one line.
  • What is new sits under a line with one Watch button. Days are marked. Your own hollers show who has seen them.
  • Games in this chat stay on their row just above the buttons, yours first.
  • Your camera is the tile in the middle, shaped like a holler that hasn't been sent yet. Tap it and it opens up to the full camera.
  • The camera and mic stay off until you tap it. Reading, watching, notes and games never switch them on or ask for them. After you close the camera they let go within a minute and a half.

Today: the camera fills the screen and the chat is a sideways strip of thumbnails at the bottom, so older things are a long swipe away and notes can't be read without opening them.

5 · Camera open

The camera, when you want it

  • Tapping your tile opens the same camera you have now: Flip, Looks, Voice, Draw, Text, More, and the record button under your thumb where the tile was.
  • Once it sends, the camera folds back into the chat and your holler lands at the bottom of the thread.
  • A game turn that needs the camera opens it for you, as it does now.
  • Holding the tile in the chat records straight away and sends when you let go.

The cost: recording a plain holler is one tap more than today (tile, then record). If that bothers you, the tile can start recording the moment you tap it instead. That is a one-line change.
