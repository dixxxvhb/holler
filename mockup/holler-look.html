The idea in one line: every chat is a solid block of its own color and pattern, and Holler is built from those blocks touching edge to edge. No cards, no blurred glass, no drop shadows. Video is the only thing that is not a block.

Three shapes do all the work: rooms are square blocks, people are circles, and anything someone sent has one sharp corner pointing at who said it.

The seven rooms. Same ids as today, so no chat changes color. Sixteen screens after today's. Give notes by number. Names and faces here are made up; the people are soft color shapes standing in for the camera.
